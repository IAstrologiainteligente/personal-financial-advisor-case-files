# Northstar Treasury Case Files

This folder contains self-created, synthetic financial materials prepared for a 13-week treasury and liquidity forecasting exercise. Northstar Equipment Services and all transactions, counterparties, names, dates, and amounts in these files are fictional.

## Files

1. [1 Bank balances.xlsx](1%20Bank%20balances.xlsx) — opening bank balances and cash restrictions.
2. [2 Accounts receivable.xlsx](2%20Accounts%20receivable.xlsx) — original customer receivable schedule.
3. [3 collection updates.xlsx](3%20collection%20updates.xlsx) — subsequent collection updates.
4. [4 Aging.xlsx](4%20Aging.xlsx) — original accounts payable aging.
5. [5 Vendor updates.xlsx](5%20Vendor%20updates.xlsx) — subsequent vendor and payable updates.
6. [6 Payroll.xlsx](6%20Payroll.xlsx) — payroll schedule.
7. [7 Taxes.xlsx](7%20Taxes.xlsx) — tax payment schedule.
8. [8 Opex.xlsx](8%20Opex.xlsx) — operating expense schedule.
9. [9. Capex .xlsx](9.%20Capex%20.xlsx) — capital expenditure commitments.
10. [10 Debt service.xlsx](10%20Debt%20service.xlsx) — debt service schedule.
11. [11 Treasure policy.xlsx](11%20Treasure%20policy.xlsx) — treasury and liquidity policy.
12. [12 Revolver terms.xlsx](12%20Revolver%20terms.xlsx) — revolving credit facility terms.
13. [13 calendar.csv](13%20calendar.csv) — business-day and bank-holiday calendar.
